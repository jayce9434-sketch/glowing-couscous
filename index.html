How Hivefall Works

Expand

Claim neutral regions touching your borders. Regions produce resources every year.

Build

Nurseries grow population, barracks grow armies, farms feed everyone, and walls strengthen defense.

Diplomacy

Trade, sign pacts, form alliances, or declare war. AI colonies remember what you do.

War

Attack connected enemy territory. Armies, local defense, structures, and species traits determine battles.
